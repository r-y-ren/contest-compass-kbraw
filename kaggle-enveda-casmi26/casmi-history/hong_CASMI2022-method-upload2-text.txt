                                               Abstract
          Critical Assessment of Small Molecule Identification (CASMI) in 2022 aims to identify
      small molecules from the increasing tandem mass (MS/MS) spectrometry database. In the
      contest, raw LC-accurate mass MS/MS data in both positive and negative mode and a list
      of unidentified small molecules are provided. A deep learning model, Mol3DNet, is trained
      to predict MS/MS from molecular 3D conformers. The training data is from the public
      libraries, including GNPS, NIST20, and MoNA. Then we established a predicted MS/MS
      library from 216,704 compounds of Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), which contains
      the spectra in different collision energies and precursor types. With the predicted library,
      the fast and sensitive searching software, msSLASH [4], is applied to identify compounds
      with their experimental mass and MS/MS. In the end, the compounds with SMILES can be
      classified by ClassyFire [1].


1     Methods
1.1    Establish predicted MS/MS library by Mol3DNet
Mol3DNet is a deep learning model predicting accurate MS/MS from molecular 3D conform-
ers. The inputs of this model is the SMILES of compounds and other experimental conditions,
including collision energy and precursor type. The SMILES is convert into 3D conformers by
Chem.MolFromSmiles() and AllChem.EmbedMolecule() in RDKit library [3], which is then
encoded into points of set. The spectra is binned into a fix-length vector, and intensities are
normalized into from 0 to 1. In Mol3DNet, a convolutional operation, MolConv, is designed to
extract features from conformers. With the sequential MolConv, the 3D compounds are embed-
ded into a vector in latent space. Then multilayer perceptron (MLP) with residual connections
decodes the vector to MS/MS. Cosine distance (equation 1) is used as loss function and metrics.

where A and B are two spectra vectors.
   Train Mol3DNet: Mol3DNet is trained on public libraries, including spectra from Global
Natural Products Social Molecular Networking (GNPS) [5], NIST20 [7], and MassBank of North
America (MoNA) [2]. We filter out spectra from Higher-energy collisional dissociation (HCD)
spectra (acquired using the ion trap MS instruments) and the combination of Q-TOF spectra
(acquired using the quadrupole/time-of-flight MS instruments) and QqQ spectra (acquired using
the triple-quadrupole MS instruments) as training datasets.

   Establish predicted library: The metabolite structures in sdf format without spectra
can be downloaded from HMDB [6] database, which are the input of Mol3DNet when inference.
Because there are tons of experimental conditions, we choose the common ones:
   • 3 level collision energy: 10 eV, 20 eV, 40 eV

   • 8 precursor types: ‘M+H’, ‘M-H’, ‘M+H-H2O’, ‘M+Na’, ‘M+H-NH3’, ‘M+H-2H2O’, ‘M-
     H-H2O’, ‘M+Cl’, ‘M+OH’
To handle the large quantity data, all the SMILES, compound mass, retention time, collision
energy, precursor type and predicted mass spectra are saved in a series of mgf format files, which
can be used as a reference library in the searching algorithm.

1.2    Identify compounds by msSLASH
The msSLASH [4] software implements a fast spectral library searching algorithm based on the
Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) technique. The reference library and query spectra are first
converted into bit-string using LSH functions. Then the similarity between the spectra with
highly similar bit-string can be computed.
   Search target MS/MS: We first process the raw MS/MS data into mgf format, including
compound mass and retention time. The codes for msSLASH is available here: https://github.
com/COL-IU/msSLASH. The setting parameters we used is shown in table 1.2.

                   Setting Parameter Description          Value
                   Minimize m/z for peaks to consider     10
                   Fragment precision in Da               0.2
                   Precursor mass tolerance in Da         2
                   Peak intensity rescale method          sqrt transformation
                   Hash functions per hash table          10
                   Iteration for searching with LSH       200

                               Table 1: The settings of msSLASH.

    The restults of seraching in predicted library is saved in Yuhui CASMI2022 pred.xlsx. In
addition to the predicted library, we also searching the spectra in assembled public libraries
including GNPS, NIST20, MoNA, and labeled HMDB. The spectra with max cosine simi-
larity among libraries is picked as final results. The resutls using second stratefy is save in
Yuhui CASMI2022 pred and pub.xlsx.
    Classify compounds: The hierarchical chemical classification of compounds is calculated
at the website of ClassyFire. The classification results, including kingdom, superclass, class, and
subclass, are organized in json format. To make the classification in a higher-order we report
the class as the compound class.
