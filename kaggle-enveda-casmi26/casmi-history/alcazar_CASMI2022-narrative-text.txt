Data was processed using SIRIUS 4.9.15 and Progenesis QI™ software with METLIN™ MS/MS Library 2019 plugin V1.0.7642.33805 (NonLinear Dynamics, Newcastle Upon Tyne, UK) and entailed peak picking, deconvolution, alignment and searching. In Progenesis QI, the automatic sensitivity was set at 3 (intermediate). ESI+, peaks were deconvoluted for [M + H]+, [M+H-H2O]+, [M+Na]+, [M+NH4]+ and [M+ACN+H]+ and for ESI- [M - H]-, [M-H+H2O]-, and [ M+Na-2H]-. Progenesis QI™ provides a score for the quality of the compound annotation, using a range from 0 to 100, with 100 being a perfect match based on the mean of multiple similarity metrics (i.e., exact mass, isotopic pattern, MS/MS data, CCS values, and retention time of authentic standards analyzed under the same conditions). The current dataset was annotated based on the accurate mass, isotope similarity, and experimental fragmentation score (ranging from 0 to 60, representing how well the observed data matches the spectral library entries). A Progenesis QI score ≥ 50 is typically reached when isotopic pattern similarity is above 90%, experimental MS/MS spectral data similarity is &gt; 50%, and the deviation of the accurate mass from the exact mass is lower than 10 ppm. Progenesis QI score ≥ 50 was considered as adequate for being considered as a candidate for putative annotation (L2 annotations according to Sumner et al.1). For each detected feature, when Progenesis didn’t reach a score&gt;50; the compounds were annotated with SIRIUS. Compound class was assessed using NP Classifier2 or ClassyFire3.
